Under sail from Rome to Venice

14 nights9 stopsWindstar Wind SpiritCruises

About this trip

Fourteen nights on a four-masted sailing yacht that carries only 148 guests. It leaves Rome, rounds the south of Italy and works up the Adriatic through Kotor, Dubrovnik and the Dalmatian islands to Venice.

Length14 nights
DestinationItaly, Montenegro and Croatia
ShipWindstar Wind Spirit
Stops9

Highlights

  • 14 nights aboard Wind Spirit, Windstar Cruises
  • All meals on board, open seating with no fixed times or tables
  • Free watersports from the stern platform: kayaking, sailing and water skiing
  • 8 ports: Naples, Messina, Kotor, Dubrovnik, Hvar, Zadar, Split and Venice
  • Embark in Rome (Civitavecchia), disembark in Venice
