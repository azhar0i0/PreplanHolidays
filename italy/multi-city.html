Multi-city is how most people actually see Italy, and it is where the planning gets fiddly: matching hotel dates to train times, working out which station, budgeting a different city tax in each place. That is the part we do. Every package below uses our four hand-checked hotels, includes breakfast every morning, and comes with a day-by-day plan that tells you which train to take and what to pay at each front desk.

Picking a route

Two cities, four to five nights. Rome and Florence is the classic long weekend for history and art. Florence and Venice is the prettiest, with a two-hour train between them. Rome and Venice gives you the two most famous cities at the cost of a longer, 3-hour 40-minute train. Milan and Venice and Milan and Florence suit anyone flying into Malpensa.

Three cities, six to seven nights. Italy's big three (Rome, Florence, Venice) is our most-booked package and the shape of most people's first Italy trip. The Northern Italy circuit swaps Rome for Milan and keeps every train under two and a half hours.

Four cities, nine nights. The grand Italian four starts in Milan and ends in Rome, so you fly into one airport and out of the other with no backtracking.

The trains

All four cities are on the Frecciarossa and Italo high-speed network. Milan to Venice takes about 2 hours 15 minutes, Venice to Florence about 2 hours, Florence to Rome 1 hour 19 minutes on the fastest service, and Milan to Florence 1 hour 45 minutes. Advance fares start around €20 per leg if you book two to three months out; flexible fares are €50 to €60. Trains leave from Milano Centrale or Porta Garibaldi, Venezia Santa Lucia, Firenze Santa Maria Novella and Roma Termini, all of which are close to our hotels. Our train guide has times, fares and which side of the platform to stand on.

How the nights are split

We give Rome three nights and the other cities two. Rome needs the extra day for the Vatican and the ancient sites; Florence and Venice each fit one full day plus two evenings very well, and Milan is a first-night city that rewards an afternoon and an aperitivo. If you want to shift nights around, tell us: every package is re-priced for custom splits.
