Venice Access Fee 2026: Dates, Price and How Hotel Guests Get Exempt

PHBy The Preplan Holidays planning team · Published September 19, 2026 · 5 min read

Venice is the first city in the world to charge day visitors an entry fee. It has run for three seasons now, and in 2026 it applied on 60 days between April and July. If you are staying overnight you do not pay it, but you do have to register. Here is everything you need, and what is still unknown for 2027.

What the fee is

The contributo di accesso is charged to visitors aged 14 and over who enter Venice's historic centre (the main island, not the Lido, Murano or Burano) on designated days between 8:30am and 4pm without staying overnight. It is separate from the overnight tourist tax that hotel guests pay.

The 2026 season

  • Dates: 60 days from 3 April to 26 July 2026: every Friday, Saturday and Sunday in that window, plus Easter Monday (6 April), the 27 to 30 April bridge and 1 to 7 June.
  • Price: €5 if booked at least four days in advance, €10 if booked later or on the day.
  • Hours: 8:30am to 4pm. Arrive after 4pm and no fee applies.
  • Where to pay: the official portal cda.ve.it, which issues a QR code that inspectors scan at the station, Piazzale Roma and the main entry points.
  • Fines: €50 to €300 for entering without a voucher or exemption.

The city reported about 680,000 paid vouchers and €5.2 million in revenue for 2026, with fewer than half paying the lower advance rate, so booking ahead matters if you are day-tripping.

Who is exempt

Overnight guests in any registered accommodation within the Comune di Venezia are exempt, as are residents, workers, students, children under 14, people visiting relatives, and visitors to the Lido and outer islands who do not enter the centre. Exemption is not automatic. Hotel guests must go to cda.ve.it, choose "Request exemption", then "I am a guest in an accommodation in Venice", enter the hotel and dates, and download the QR exemption voucher. Hotels are obliged to tell you this, and we include the link and instructions in every Venice voucher pack.

Do it before you travelRegister as soon as your booking is confirmed. Inspectors do check at Santa Lucia station on fee days, and "my hotel will confirm it" does not help you at the barrier.

What about 2027?

The 2026 calendar was approved in November 2025, so expect the 2027 dates and prices to be announced in late 2026. The council has signalled that the fee will continue; whether the price or number of days changes is not yet known. We will update this page, and your voucher pack if you have booked, as soon as the decision is published.

Other Venice rules worth knowing

  • Tour groups are capped at 25 people and loudspeakers are banned, which has made the centre noticeably calmer since 2024.
  • The overnight tourist tax is €1 to €5 per person per night by star rating, for up to five nights, paid at the hotel. See our Italy tourist tax guide.
  • Large cruise ships no longer sail past St Mark's; they dock at Marghera on the mainland.
