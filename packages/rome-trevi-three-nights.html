About this trip

A central Rome base for the Colosseum, the Vatican and late walks past the fountain. The quadruple room suits families and friends sharing.

Highlights

  • 3 nights at Trevi Collection Hotel, near the Trevi Fountain

  • Every city tax and hotel policy explained before you pay

Day by day itinerary

1

Land at Rome Fiumicino (FCO) or arrive by train at Roma Termini. Check in at Trevi Collection Hotel, near the Trevi Fountain. Your standard quadruple room is prepaid with breakfast.

2

Start early at the Colosseum, then walk the Forum and Palatine Hill. Evening coin toss at Trevi, a few minutes from the hotel.

3

Book a morning slot for the Sistine Chapel, then cross to St Peter's Basilica. Dinner in Trastevere.

4

Enjoy one last breakfast, settle any city tax and extras at the front desk, and check out of Trevi Collection Hotel.

Dates and prices

From $489 per person, two sharing

Hotel rates are the same year round for the standard room. Peak dates (Easter, late April to June, September and October) sell out first, so book six to twelve weeks ahead. Children aged 2 to 17 sharing a room with two adults are priced at 60% of the adult rate.
