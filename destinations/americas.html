When to go

November to April is dry and comfortable on the Yucatán coast and mild in Mexico City, where the altitude keeps evenings cool year round. May to October brings afternoon rain and, on the coast, sargassum seaweed in some months; the hotels and we monitor it. Day of the Dead (1 to 2 November) is extraordinary in Mexico City and books out months ahead.

Entry for US passport holders

A valid passport and the free FMM tourist form, now completed digitally on arrival at most airports. No visa for stays under 180 days.
