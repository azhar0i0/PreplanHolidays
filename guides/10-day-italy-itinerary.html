10-Day Italy Itinerary: Milan, Venice, Florence and Rome

PHBy The Preplan Holidays planning team · Published September 19, 2026 · 8 min read

With nine or ten nights you can do the whole spine of Italy in one direction. This is the plan behind our grand Italian four: two nights in Milan, two in Venice, two in Florence, three in Rome. Fly into Malpensa, out of Fiumicino, and every train runs south.

Days 1 and 2: Milan

Malpensa Express to Porta Garibaldi (about 50 minutes) and walk to the hotel next door. First afternoon: metro to the Duomo, the roof terraces at golden hour, the Galleria, La Scala, dinner in Brera. Day 2: the Last Supper at your reserved time (tickets sell in four-month blocks; reserve as soon as your dates are fixed), Sforza Castle, Parco Sempione, then an evening on the Navigli canals. If you would rather, swap the afternoon for a train to Lake Como: Varenna is an hour from Centrale.

Days 3 and 4: Venice

Pay the Milan city tax (€10 per adult per night in 2026), then Frecciarossa or Italo to Venezia Santa Lucia, 2 hours 15 minutes. Vaporetto down the Grand Canal to San Marco. Follow days 6 and 7 of our 7-day itinerary: the square in the evening, St Mark's and the Doge's Palace the next morning, islands or wandering in the afternoon.

Days 5 and 6: Florence

Venice city tax (€4.50 per adult per night), then the train to Firenze SMN, about 2 hours. Duomo and Ponte Vecchio on arrival; Uffizi, Mercato Centrale and Piazzale Michelangelo on the full day. Reserve the Uffizi a month ahead and the Accademia two months ahead.

Days 7, 8 and 9: Rome

Florence city tax (€7 per adult per night), then 1 hour 19 minutes to Roma Termini. Evening at the Trevi Fountain and the Pantheon. Day 8: Colosseum, Forum and Palatine, then Trastevere. Day 9: Vatican Museums and St Peter's, then the Borghese Gallery or a long lunch in Prati. Rome's city tax is €7.50 per adult per night at a four-star and is paid when you leave.

Day 10: Home

Leonardo Express to Fiumicino (32 minutes) or the €55 fixed-fare taxi. Allow three hours before departure.

What it costs

The grand Italian four hotel package with breakfast is from $1,641 per person, two sharing. Budget around €130 per couple for city taxes across the four cities, €60 to €80 per person for three advance train tickets, and about €110 per person for the Last Supper, St Mark's, Uffizi, Colosseum and Vatican reservations. If you would rather have flights, trains, transfers and guided tours bundled, Italy end to end by fast train covers the same four cities in ten nights from $2,190 with return flights from the USA.

Two things that make it run smoothly

  • Open-jaw flights. Search "multi-city" on the airline site: New York to Milan, Rome to New York. It rarely costs more than a return and saves a three-hour train back north.
  • One suitcase each. You will lift it onto three trains and one boat. Milan and Rome hotels will hold bags if you arrive before check-in.
