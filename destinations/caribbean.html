Most Caribbean packages drop you at one resort for a week. Ours move. In Jamaica you get three nights each on the north coast at Montego Bay and Ocho Rios and three on the west at Negril, with every transfer between them included, so you see Dunn's River Falls, the Blue Mountains and the Seven Mile Beach sunset without a single rental car. In the Dominican Republic you split time between Santo Domingo's cobbled Zona Colonial and the palm-lined beaches of Las Terrenas.

When to go

December to April is the dry, busy season across the Caribbean. May, June and November are quieter with short showers and lower hotel rates. Hurricane season runs June to November and peaks in September; we build refundable options into late-summer bookings and never book what we would not book for ourselves.

Entry for US passport holders

Jamaica requires a passport and a completed online immigration form before arrival; no visa. The Dominican Republic requires a passport and the free e-ticket form, completed online before you fly. Flights from the eastern US are short: three to four hours from New York or Miami.
