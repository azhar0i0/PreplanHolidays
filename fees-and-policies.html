City taxes and hotel policies, hotel by hotel

Every hotel has its own rules. Here is what each one actually means for you before you pay, plus the 2026 city-tax rates for all four cities.

Trevi Collection Hotel

Via Capo le Case 60, 00187 Rome, Italy
Standard Quadruple Room, breakfast. Double bed requested.
Rome packages and guide

City tax

Tourist city tax is not in the price. If it applies, you pay it on the spot at the hotel.

Deposit

The hotel may ask for a cash or card deposit as a guarantee. It comes back at departure, minus any charges.

Travelling with a baby

A cot for children aged 0 to 2 costs 16 euros per night, paid at the hotel.

Groups

Five or more rooms on the same dates count as a group. The hotel confirms these separately, at its own rates.

Grand Hotel Adriatico

Via Maso Finiguerra 9, 50123 Florence, Italy
Comfort Room, breakfast. Double bed requested.
Florence packages and guide

City tax

Local city tax and any resort fee are not in the rate. You pay them directly to the hotel.

Late arrival

The front desk is open 24 hours, so a late train or flight is not a problem.

Bed type

A double bed is requested for you. The hotel does its best, but bedding is not guaranteed.

No-show

Same-day cancellations and no-shows are charged 100% of the stay.

Hotel Monaco & Grand Canal

Calle Vallaresso, San Marco 1332, 30124 Venice, Italy
Standard Room, breakfast. Bed preference passed on.
Venice packages and guide

City tax

Set by star rating and season, up to 5 euros per person per night from February to December, 30% less in January. Charged for the first 5 nights only.

Children

Under 10s pay no city tax. Ages 10 to 16 pay half.

Dates are fixed

Once booked, the check-in date on this stay cannot be changed.

No-show

Same-day cancellations and no-shows are charged 100% of the stay.

Holiday Inn Milan Garibaldi Station

Via Ugo Bassi 1/a, 20159 Milan, Italy
Double Standard Room, breakfast for 2. Twin beds requested.
Milan packages and guide

City tax

10 euros per person, per night, paid at the hotel. Guests under 18 are exempt.

At check-in

Every guest, children included, shows a government photo ID or passport.

Breakfast

Covers up to 2 adults sharing a room. Extra guests pay for breakfast at the hotel.

Paying locally

Cashless payment works for everything. Cash payments are capped at 5,000 euros by Italian law.

Rules that apply at every hotel

  • ID for everyone. Italian law requires hotels to register every guest, including children, with the police. Bring a passport or government photo ID for each traveller and expect an in-person check at the desk.
  • No-shows and same-day cancellations are charged 100% of the stay.
  • Bed types are requested, never guaranteed. We pass your preference on at booking and again a few days before arrival.
  • Extras such as minibar, parking, late check-out and room upgrades are paid at the hotel.
  • Problems should be raised with the hotel immediately and then with us on WhatsApp. Issues reported after the stay are much harder to resolve.

Preplan's own fees

There are none beyond the package price. Optional add-ons are priced on each package page: airport pickup $38 per person, travel insurance $24 per person, high-speed train tickets $45 per person per leg, room upgrade on request about $40 per room per night. Card payments carry no surcharge.
