Asia is where a package earns its keep. Internal flights, ferry timetables, multiple languages and visa rules make independent planning a full-time job; we do it once, properly, and hand you a single itinerary. The Thailand and Malaysia packages include international flights from the USA. The China tour is land only, which lets you use points on the long-haul and which we can price with flights on request.

When to go

Thailand's dry season runs November to April; the Andaman coast (Phuket, Phi Phi) is best from November to March. Malaysia and Singapore are warm and humid year round, with Borneo driest from March to October. China is best in April to May and September to October, avoiding the Golden Week holiday in the first week of October when the whole country travels.

Visas for US passport holders

Thailand, Malaysia and Singapore all allow visa-free entry for US citizens for tourism (Thailand for 60 days). China requires a visa for most US travellers, applied for in advance; a 240-hour visa-free transit exists for some routings, and we advise on which applies to your itinerary.
