About this trip

Day by day itinerary

1

Land at Milan Malpensa (MXP) or arrive by train at Milano Porta Garibaldi / Milano Centrale. Check in at Holiday Inn Milan Garibaldi Station, by Garibaldi Station. Your double standard room is prepaid with breakfast for 2.

2

Take the lift to the cathedral terraces, browse Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II, then aperitivo along the Navigli canals.

3

Enjoy one last breakfast, settle any city tax and extras at the front desk, and check out of Holiday Inn Milan Garibaldi Station.
