Day by day itinerary

1

Land at Venice Marco Polo (VCE) or arrive by train at Venezia Santa Lucia. Check in at Hotel Monaco & Grand Canal, on the Grand Canal by St Mark's. Your standard room is prepaid with breakfast.

2

Basilica and Doge's Palace in the morning, then ride vaporetto line 1 the full length of the Grand Canal. Spritz by the water.

3

Enjoy one last breakfast, settle any city tax and extras at the front desk, and check out of Hotel Monaco & Grand Canal.

Dates and prices

From $612 per person, two sharing

Hotel rates are the same year round for the standard room. Peak dates (Easter, late April to June, September and October) sell out first, so book six to twelve weeks ahead. Children aged 2 to 17 sharing a room with two adults are priced at 60% of the adult rate.
