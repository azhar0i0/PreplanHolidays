Day by day itinerary

1

Land at Rome Fiumicino (FCO) or arrive by train at Roma Termini. Check in at Trevi Collection Hotel, near the Trevi Fountain. Your standard quadruple room is prepaid with breakfast.

2

Start early at the Colosseum, then walk the Forum and Palatine Hill. Evening coin toss at Trevi, a few minutes from the hotel.

3

Book a morning slot for the Sistine Chapel, then cross to St Peter's Basilica. Dinner in Trastevere.

4

Breakfast, check out, then take the high-speed train to Florence (Firenze Santa Maria Novella). Check in at Grand Hotel Adriatico, near Santa Maria Novella. Your comfort room is prepaid with breakfast.

5

Climb Brunelleschi's dome in the morning, then spend the afternoon with Botticelli at the Uffizi. Sunset from Piazzale Michelangelo.

6

Enjoy one last breakfast, settle any city tax and extras at the front desk, and check out of Grand Hotel Adriatico.
