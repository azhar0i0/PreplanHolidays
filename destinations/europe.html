Our European packages are built for one trip of a lifetime rather than a checklist. Each one spends enough nights in each place to see it properly, uses hotels we would stay in ourselves, and includes the guided visits that are genuinely better with a guide (the Acropolis, the Colosseum, Versailles) while leaving free days for the rest.

What every Europe package includes

  • Return international flights from the USA, with departures from major gateways such as New York, Chicago, Los Angeles and Dallas (departure city changes the price, and we quote yours)
  • All hotel nights with breakfast
  • Every transfer between airport, hotel, station and port
  • The guided tours listed on each package page
  • 24/7 support by WhatsApp while you travel

Not included: travel insurance (offered when you book), meals not listed, local city taxes where they apply, and personal spending.

Entry rules for US travellers in 2026

Greece, Italy, France and Portugal are all in the Schengen area: no visa for stays up to 90 days, a passport valid for three months beyond your departure, and since April 2026 a biometric registration at first entry under the Entry/Exit System. The ETIAS authorisation is expected to start in 2027 and is not needed for 2026 travel. Turkey, on our Middle East page, has separate rules.
