We book three cruises because they are the three we would take ourselves. Windstar's Wind Spirit is a four-masted yacht that carries fewer people than a large hotel and slips into harbours like Kotor and Hvar that big ships cannot enter. MSC Sinfonia gives you Palermo, Valencia, Barcelona, Marseille and Genoa for less than most people spend on a week of hotels. Norwegian Encore does Glacier Bay with a go-kart track on top.

Cruise and stay

Both Mediterranean cruises start in Rome (the port is Civitavecchia, an hour by train or transfer from the city). Add our three nights by the Trevi Fountain before you sail, or, for the Adriatic voyage that ends in Venice, add two nights on the Grand Canal at the end. We price the hotel, the transfer to the port and the cruise as one booking.

How cruise fares work

Fares are per person based on two sharing the lowest cabin grade on a specific sailing date, and they move with demand. The prices on our pages are the lowest we have seen for each ship; we check live availability and confirm the exact fare, cabin and deposit before you pay. Gratuities, drinks packages and shore excursions are extra unless the offer on the package page says otherwise. Ask us about refund terms before booking; cruise lines' cancellation windows are stricter than hotels'.
