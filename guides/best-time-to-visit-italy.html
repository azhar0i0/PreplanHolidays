Best Time to Visit Italy: Rome, Florence, Venice and Milan Month by Month

PHBy The Preplan Holidays planning team · Published September 19, 2026 · 6 min read

The short answer is April to early June and September to October. The longer answer depends on what you are trading off: heat against crowds, price against daylight, and a handful of specific dates that can double a hotel rate. Here is how the year looks in the four cities we book.

The best months

April and May bring mild days (15 to 24°C), long evenings and gardens in flower, but also Easter week, the 25 April and 1 May holidays and, in Milan, the Salone del Mobile design fair, all of which fill hotels. Book three months out. September and October are the planners' favourite: summer heat fades, the sea is still warm, the crowds thin after the first week of September and prices ease from mid-October. Milan Fashion Week in late September is the exception.

June to August

June is still pleasant, especially in Venice and Milan. July and August are hot: Rome and Florence regularly pass 35°C and the queues at open-air sites are punishing by 11am. August is also when Italians go on holiday. Around Ferragosto (15 August) many family-run restaurants and shops close for one to three weeks, though the Colosseum, Vatican Museums, Uffizi and other state museums stay open. The upside is that city hotels discount heavily in August, and if you plan early mornings and late evenings with a siesta between, Rome in August can be a bargain.

November to March

This is the cheap, quiet season. Museums have no lines, hotels drop rates by a third or more, and Rome in particular is mild (10 to 15°C by day). Venice has two things to know: the risk of acqua alta flooding in St Mark's Square from November to January, now much reduced by the MOSE barriers, and Carnival in the two weeks before Lent (February in 2027), when the city is spectacular and hotel prices double. Florence is cold and can be grey but is lovely at Christmas. Milan in winter is about opera, shopping and fog.

City by city

  • Rome: April, May, October. Avoid August afternoons and Easter week unless you are going for them.
  • Florence: May, June, September. Reserve the Uffizi and Accademia whenever you go between April and October.
  • Venice: late April to June, September, October. March is a good compromise: cool, cheap and uncrowded. Check the access fee calendar if you are day-tripping, though hotel guests are exempt.
  • Milan: April to June, September, October, avoiding the fairs. Lake Como is best from May to September.

How far ahead to book

For April to June and September to October, book hotels six to twelve weeks ahead; our Venice hotel fills first. For the specific dates above, book three months out. For winter, a few weeks is usually plenty, except Carnival and Christmas. Flights from the US are cheapest booked three to five months ahead for spring and autumn travel.
