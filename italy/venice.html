Venice rewards the people who stay overnight. Day-trippers see St Mark's Square at its busiest and leave before the light goes; hotel guests get the square at 7am, the Grand Canal after dark and, since 2024, an exemption from the city's day-visitor access fee. Our hotel is on the canal itself, at the San Marco end, so you step out of the lobby onto the water.

Where you stay: Hotel Monaco & Grand Canal

Hotel Monaco & Grand Canal is on Calle Vallaresso in the San Marco district, facing Santa Maria della Salute across the water. We book the Standard Room with breakfast for two included and pass on your bed preference to the hotel. The Grand Canal terrace restaurant is where you will want your morning coffee. The San Marco Vallaresso vaporetto stop is beside the hotel, so line 1 along the whole Grand Canal and the boats to the Lido and the airport are all outside the door.

Two rules worth reading twice. Once booked, the check-in date on this stay cannot be changed, so confirm your travel dates before we lock it in. And same-day cancellations and no-shows are charged 100% of the stay.

Venice city tax in 2026

Venice charges an overnight imposta di soggiorno that depends on the hotel's star rating, its location and the season. In the historic centre in high season the hotel rates are:

Accommodation (historic centre)Per person, per night
5-star hotel€5.00
4-star hotel€4.50
3-star hotel€3.50
2-star hotel€2.00
1-star hotel€1.00

It is charged for a maximum of 5 consecutive nights, reduced by 30% in January, and reduced by 30% on the mainland at Mestre and 20% on the outer islands. Children under 10 pay nothing and ages 10 to 16 pay half. Pay it at the hotel at check-out.

The Venice access fee and why hotel guests do not pay it

Separately from the overnight tax, Venice charges day visitors a contributo di accesso on peak days. In 2026 it applied on 60 days between 3 April and 26 July, mostly Fridays to Sundays plus holiday bridges, from 8:30am to 4pm: €5 if paid at least four days ahead, €10 otherwise, for anyone aged 14 or over entering the historic centre. Guests staying overnight in Venice are exempt, but you must register on the city's cda.ve.it portal, choose "I am a guest in an accommodation in Venice", enter your dates and download the QR exemption voucher. Inspectors do check at the station and the Piazzale Roma, and fines run from €50 to €300. We send you the link with your vouchers. The 2027 calendar had not been published when we last checked; see our access fee guide for the latest.

Getting in

By train, Venezia Santa Lucia is the station on the island (do not get off at Venezia Mestre). From there, vaporetto line 1 or 2 down the Grand Canal reaches San Marco Vallaresso in 25 to 45 minutes; a single ticket is €9.50, so a 24-hour pass at €25 usually pays for itself. From Marco Polo airport (VCE) the Alilaguna Blue and Orange lines run to San Marco in about 75 minutes for €18 one way (children under 6 free), or a shared water taxi takes 30 minutes for around €35 per person. A private water taxi to the hotel door is about €130 for the boat.

What to book before you fly

  • St Mark's Basilica now uses timed, named tickets from the official site: €10 for the basilica, €30 for the full visit with the Pala d'Oro and the museum terrace, under-11s free. Book a few days ahead in peak season.
  • Doge's Palace and the Secret Itineraries tour are worth reserving online to skip the ticket line.
  • Gondola rides have an official tariff: €90 for 30 minutes by day, €110 after 7pm, per boat for up to five people. There is nothing to book; walk to any stand.

How to spend two nights

Arrive by boat and give the first evening to the square and the Riva degli Schiavoni after the day-trippers have left. Next morning, the basilica at opening time, then the Doge's Palace, then ride vaporetto line 1 the whole length of the Grand Canal to the station and walk back through Cannaregio and the Rialto market. Evening spritz on a Dorsoduro canal. A second full day is for Murano and Burano, or simply for getting lost, which is the point of Venice.

When to go

April to June and September to October are the sweet spots. Carnival, in the two weeks before Lent (February in 2027), doubles hotel prices. November to January brings the risk of acqua alta in St Mark's Square, although the MOSE barriers now hold back the worst tides, and January has the cheapest rooms and a 30% city-tax discount. Remember that tour groups are capped at 25 people and loudspeakers are banned, which has made the centre calmer than it was.
